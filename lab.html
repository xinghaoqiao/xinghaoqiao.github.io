About SIBEL

At SIBEL, we develop statistical theory and methods to address problems in business and economics. Our research spans causal inference and experimental design, complex time series analysis, and the statistical foundations of AI. We connect methodological innovation with applications in digital experimentation, economic and financial modelling and forecasting, and investigate how AI-generated data can improve statistical inference and learning.

Research Directions

Learning from Interventions

We develop methods for causal inference and experimental design, motivated by digital A/B testing and decision-making in business and economics.

Learning over Time

We study complex time series to understand temporal dynamics and improve economic and financial modelling and forecasting.

Learning with AI

We study the statistical foundations of AI and how AI-generated data can improve statistical inference and learning.
